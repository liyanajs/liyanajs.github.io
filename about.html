About Me.

I’m Liyana. Finding logic in the labyrinth.

My path to engineering wasn't a straight line—it was a map I drew myself. It started in the world of GIS, using Python to convert files and build web maps. That sparked a curiosity for the web that led me to front-end development at a fast-paced startup, where I truly fell in love with code.


Most recently, I worked in the gaming industry, experimenting freely across the full stack and diving into server engineering over the past year. I love the process of figuring out what works (and what definitely doesn’t) and carrying those lessons forward.


Currently, I’m looking for my next role in Front-End or Full-Stack Engineering. I’m open to exploring diverse domains and industries—what matters most to me is solving meaningful problems and contributing to a team that values continuous learning and solid execution.


Outside of the terminal? I’m usually painting, nurturing my plants, or capturing life through my camera lens.
